# Product Vision

## Problem

People frequently receive a PDF that must be completed, signed, corrected, or returned immediately. Existing tools often require accounts, uploads, subscriptions, watermarks, or complex workflows.

## Vision

Provide the fastest private path from receiving a PDF to sending back a completed PDF.

## Product statement

> Open a PDF, complete it, download it, and leave. The document never leaves your browser.

## Target scenarios

- Complete an application or authorization form.
- Add a signature and date.
- Place initials on several pages.
- Add checkmarks or crosses.
- Type into a non-fillable PDF.
- Correct a visible mistake using whiteout and replacement text.
- Rotate or remove an unnecessary page.
- Combine a supporting page with the main PDF.
- Download the completed file for email or upload elsewhere.

## Product principles

- Immediate usefulness.
- No account.
- No upload.
- No document retention.
- No watermark.
- No hidden premium boundary.
- Clear limitations.
- Accessible on common devices.
