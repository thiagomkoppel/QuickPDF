## Tablet

Inspector collapses.

Larger touch targets.

## Mobile

Toolbar moves to bottom.

Properties become bottom sheets.

Drag

Tap

# Accessibility

Touch targets

Minimum 44px

No hover-only actions.

Everything reachable from keyboard.

---

Portrait and landscape must both work.

Do not force one orientation.
